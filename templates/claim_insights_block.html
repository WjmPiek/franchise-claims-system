Additional claims scenarios

Early incidents by product (within 180 days of entry), reporting-to-payment delay by franchise, and missing data are included as clickable cards. Further useful comparisons are repudiation reasons, claim frequency per 1,000 insured lives and repeat claims per policy. Claim frequency requires a matching insured-life exposure history; no substitute denominator is used.
