Import the two approved source formats

Use the annual Martins Funeral All Claims workbook for claims and the monthly PolicyData_DTSTAMP_RECEIPTSFOR source for policies. The October example defines the policy format; import each required month's matching file. These two sources supply calculations, analytics and the approved reports.

The claims workbook is split automatically by Date Of Claim Paid (Y). All actual paid months are retained, including payments outside the filename's reporting window. AM supplies bank-paid claims, AL remains separate, and approved/repudiated/unpaid cases remain in source analytics. Original files and previous versions are archived; a successful monthly replacement does not add duplicate current rows.

Insurer, pay-over, product-ratio, voucher and branch calculation files are outputs, not additional imports. Files process in the background. Failures preserve current data and do not stop subsequent files.
